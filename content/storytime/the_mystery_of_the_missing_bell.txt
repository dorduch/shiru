Every morning, the bakery bell jingled when the door opened. One Tuesday, it was gone. Baker Jo looked under the counter. Niko checked every shelf. No bell.

Niko noticed three clues: a silver thread on the doormat, a trail of floury dots, and a faint jingle from the courtyard. He followed the dots past the mixing bowls and out to the herb garden.

There sat a magpie, carefully weaving the silver bell into a nest. Niko did not shout or grab. He placed a shiny bottle cap nearby and waited. The magpie tilted its head, chose the bottle cap, and left the bell on the wall.

Baker Jo cleaned the bell and tied it above the door with a stronger ribbon. Jingle!

The mystery was solved without frightening anyone. Niko wrote the clues in his notebook and added a final detective rule: look closely, move gently, and remember that even a mystery can have a kind answer.
