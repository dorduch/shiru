Nia planted six sunflower seeds in a patch beside her home. Every morning she carried a small green watering can outside and whispered, “Grow when you are ready.”

One seedling bent after a rainy night. Nia made it a tiny support from two twigs. Another seedling was shaded by a large leaf, so she moved a pot to give it light.

Soon, neighbors began stopping by. Mr. Lee brought compost. Amara painted cheerful plant signs. Little Sam could not carry a watering can, so Nia gave him a cup and a very important corner to water.

By summer, six bright sunflowers stood taller than everyone. Nia saved their seeds in paper envelopes and gave one to each helper.

The next spring, sunflowers appeared in gardens all along the street. Nia's small patch had grown into something much bigger, not because the flowers traveled, but because kindness did.
