Two villages stood on opposite sides of a bright little river. The children could wave to each other, but the old wooden bridge had lost three boards in a storm.

Mara brought a long plank, but it was too heavy to lift alone. Theo brought rope, but he did not know the strongest knot. Sumi knew the knot, and Ben knew how to keep everyone safely back while the grown-ups checked the work.

At first, each child tried to solve everything alone. The plank thumped down. The rope tangled. Everyone became grumpy.

Then Mara said, “What if we each do the part we know?” Theo and Sumi tied the rope. Mara and two friends carried the plank together. Ben counted each careful step. The village carpenter secured the final board.

By sunset, the bridge was ready. The children met in the middle and shared warm berry buns. The bridge was made of wood and rope, but everyone knew the strongest part was the helping hands on both sides.
