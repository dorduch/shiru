Luna liked to say goodnight to everything. Goodnight slippers. Goodnight lamp. Goodnight blue cup by the bed. But one evening, the moon outside her window looked wide awake.

“Perhaps the moon forgot how to get sleepy,” Luna whispered. She tucked a silver blanket around her toy rabbit and began a quiet bedtime tour. She showed the moon how the curtains swayed slowly. She listened to the soft hum of the night breeze. She counted five calm breaths, in and out.

The moon seemed to follow. A cloud drifted over its bright face like a pillow. The stars blinked more slowly. Luna told the moon about one happy part of her day, then one thing she could leave until tomorrow.

Soon Luna's eyes grew heavy. The moon became a gentle glow behind its cloud blanket. “Goodnight, moon,” Luna murmured.

The room was still. The night was safe. Luna curled beside her rabbit, breathed one more slow breath, and floated into a peaceful dream.
