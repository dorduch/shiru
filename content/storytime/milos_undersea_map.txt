Milo the young dolphin found a map tucked inside a bottle. It showed a path through the coral garden to a place marked with a golden shell.

Milo invited his friend Tavi the turtle. They followed three bubble rings, turned beside the purple coral, and swam under an arch where silver fish shimmered like stars. When the current pushed them off course, Tavi noticed a tiny arrow carved into a rock.

The map led to a quiet cave. Inside, they found no pile of gold. Instead, there was a circle of smooth shells and a note: “The treasure is a place worth sharing.”

Milo and Tavi laughed. They decorated the cave with sea flowers, then invited every neighbor they met. Soon the hidden cave was filled with songs, gentle games, and new friends.

Milo kept the map, but he added one new mark beside the golden shell: a little heart, so the next explorer would know what kind of treasure waited there.
