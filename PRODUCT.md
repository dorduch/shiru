# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Storytime serves children ages 3–10 who listen independently and the parents
who create the account, configure one child profile, and manage safety and data.

## Product Purpose

Storytime lets a child build a safe narrated story from visual choices, resume
it later, and listen again without ads, feeds, or reading-heavy navigation.
The child profile and listening library stay encrypted on the device. Only an
age band and curated story choices are sent to the cloud for generation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, magical, playful, and trustworthy. Pastel surfaces and expressive pixel
art provide warmth without creating an overstimulating entertainment feed.

## Design Principles

1. Child actions are visual, spoken, large, and reversible.
2. Parent settings remain behind the existing adult and PIN gates.
3. Never send a child’s name, story text, or listening history to analytics.
4. Generated content fails closed when safety checks are inconclusive.
5. Motion communicates state and respects reduced-motion preferences.
